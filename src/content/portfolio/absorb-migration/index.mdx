## The Challenge

The existing LMS at Deseret First Credit Union couldn't keep up with the business needs and evolving employee learning model. DFCU had recently changed from a traditional teller and loan officer model to a Member Service Representative with levels ranging from MSR I (a more traditional teller role) to MSR IV (a loan officer and assistant manager position).

With the current LMS, reporting was lacking and the customization of learning paths was non-existant.

  ### Constraints
  - No additional yearly budget was allocated for the LMS—whichever new system was implemented needed to match the cost of the current system.
  - Custom learning paths and SCORM support were required.
  - Advanced reporting features and automated reporting were required.
  
</CalloutFrame>

After compiling the data, Absorb surfaced as the clearest candidate. The data and recommendation to implement Absorb as the new LMS was presented to Executive Leadership.

The total migration timeline was set for two months. This allowed for the existing LMS data and content to be imported into Absorb. Annual compliance course data and historic course completion was preserved.

## The Solution

Implementation of Absorb LMS. Shared reporting was built to track learner progress and milestones. Automated notifications set up for both learners and field admins to notify of any due actions or courses awaiting completion.

## Outcome

### Learner Engagement
  - Annual compliance on-time completion increased by 5% with the increased automation and learner notifications.
  - Custom billboards to feature courses.
  - Reporting automations and robustness allowed additional manager involvement and coaching opportunities.

### Admin Capabilities
  - Time spent compiling manual reports decreased significantly.
  - Increased clarity around learner enrollments and next steps.

### Sustainability
  - Custom learning paths set up for additional roles.
  - LMS still in use after leaving for another opportunity.
  - Pricing negotiated to accomodate for growth in workforce without significant increase.
